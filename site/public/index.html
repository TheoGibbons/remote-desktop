Private by design

The relay connects you.
It can’t read your screen.

Screen frames, input, file names, and file contents are encrypted between your paired devices. The relay only forwards ciphertext and connection metadata.

Read the protocol ↗
  1. 1
    Pair with a secret

    A long session key creates the private meeting point for your devices.

  2. 2
    Approve each device

    Compare a short device code before granting persistent trust.

  3. 3
    Stay encrypted

    AES-256-GCM protects content in transit, including through the relay.
